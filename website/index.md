The work runs in three strands: curating multilingual datasets for domains
where data is sensitive or scarce, designing the model architecture and the
tokenizer that feeds it, and measuring what comes out against much larger
models. Everything it produces — models, datasets and training code — is
published openly with documentation as the work proceeds.
[More about the project](about.md).

## What the project publishes

Everything the project produces is public as it is made, not at the end.
